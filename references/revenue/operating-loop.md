# Operating loop

Use this reference to diagnose where revenue is blocked and route work to the right operating module.

## Lifecycle gates

| Stage | Required exit evidence | Next stage |
|---|---|---|
| Signal captured | A dated source, raw customer language, context, and problem signal | Problem validation |
| Problem validated | Repeated or high-intensity evidence, a defined segment/situation, current workaround, and a falsifiable hypothesis | Offer design |
| Offer ready | Clear buyer, promise, inclusions/exclusions, delivery path, proof requirement, cost floor, and price hypothesis | Qualified opportunity |
| Qualified opportunity | Confirmed problem, consequence, decision participants, decision process, timing, and a dated next step | Proposal |
| Proposal active | Buyer-specific success criteria, scope, exclusions, price, acceptance, decision plan, and acknowledged next step | Won/lost |
| Closed won | Explicit acceptance plus agreed commercial and delivery start conditions | Onboarding |
| Activated | Sales-to-delivery handoff completed and first measurable value achieved | Delivery/value realization |
| Delivered | Acceptance criteria met, exceptions recorded, and payment/closure status known | Success/renewal |
| Value proven | Outcome evidence reviewed with the customer and next success goal agreed | Renewal/expansion/referral |
| Renewed/expanded/referred | Explicit new commitment or qualified referral | New lifecycle loop |

Do not advance a stage because a document was produced. Advance when the buyer or customer provides the required evidence or commitment.

## Diagnostic sequence

1. Identify the last stage with sufficient exit evidence.
2. Find the first missing gate after it.
3. Determine whether the gap is evidence, economics, trust, authority, delivery capacity, or timing.
4. Design the smallest action that can close or invalidate the gap.
5. Record the result and route to the next stage.

## Weekly operator review

- Customer evidence: what new signal changed a hypothesis?
- Opportunities: which have no dated buyer-owned next step?
- Proposals: which decision is blocked, by whom, and by what evidence?
- Delivery: what is at risk against scope, date, acceptance, or payment?
- Customer success: which account has declining value, engagement, or renewal confidence?
- Economics: which offer, channel, or client consumes more cash/time than expected?
- Capacity: what should be standardized, automated, delegated, stopped, or repriced?

End with at most three priorities. Each needs an owner, date, expected result, and record to update.
