# Source provenance and audit notes

This skill contains an original, condensed operating design informed by these audited public repositories:

- `ericrisco/rsc-harness` — MIT. Informed pipeline hygiene, proposals/SOW boundaries, onboarding gates, pricing arithmetic, unit economics, and retention routing. Fixed benchmark values, unsupported performance claims, universal time thresholds, and jurisdiction-specific legal conclusions were deliberately excluded or converted into company-calibrated rules.
- `Infinite-Labs-AI/infinite-skills` — MIT. Informed customer research, offer design, and sales decision-asset structure. Selected upstream skills may also be installed separately.
- `lowwwbank/interview-to-jtbd` — MIT. Informed evidence traceability, JTBD synthesis, opportunity maps, and confidence handling. The upstream skill may also be installed separately.
- `t0ddc3by/claude-for-customer-success` — Apache-2.0. Informed customer-success coverage, renewal risk, expansion, and operating handoffs; Claude-specific configuration and tooling were not copied.
